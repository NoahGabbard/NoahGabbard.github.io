Process Improvement

Non-Conveyable Market Sorting System

Identified a systemic inefficiency in the non-conveyable department where packages across different markets were loaded indiscriminately into ULDs, creating downstream confusion and slowing overall throughput. I took the lead in designing a market-based sorting solution — using visual markers and dedicated carts per market — and collaborated with a fellow associate to execute it on the floor. I then proposed the designation of an official staging area to our Area Manager, which gave the team a consistent visual reference and a repeatable process structure where none had existed before. Based on that improvement, our Area Manager recognized our work and connected us with the regional senior safety manager, giving us the opportunity to walk through the process changes directly and explain the operational and safety impact. The sorting method we introduced remains the department standard to this day.

We became directly responsible for ensuring cardboard was properly palletized and moved to the correct areas, and that workflow remains an active department process today. The initiative reflected a personal commitment to ownership beyond the defined scope of my role and set a visible behavioral standard for floor upkeep within the team.
